## TL;DR

- Built SEO as a product workstream, not a marketing function, across three pillars that each needed the others to work: editorial blogs, Bond Dictionary, and programmatic ISIN pages.
- Shipped 350+ blogs, 200+ dictionary terms, and 26,000+ programmatic pages inside 3 months by removing the SEBI compliance bottleneck with a Claude-powered review agent.
- Grew daily organic clicks ~2x in 3 months and started ranking for ISIN-level and high-intent non-brand queries like "hedge funds in india" at #3.

## Context

Performance ads were doing the bulk of acquisition at BondScanner. Expensive, non-compounding, and tied to monthly spend. Organic was the obvious lever, but no retail bond platform in India had built real SEO depth in this category.

The pitch to the founder was simple. Every blog, dictionary term, and programmatic page we publish today keeps working for years. Ads stop the moment we stop paying. SEO is a moat if we commit to it.

## Approach

I ran it as a three-pillar product, because any single pillar on its own wouldn't have worked.

Editorial content gives you the E-E-A-T authority Google needs to trust the domain. The Bond Dictionary is Investopedia-style, anchored to the high-intent keywords retail investors actually search. Programmatic ISIN pages give you scale: one page per bond, each targeting long-tail bond-specific queries.

Programmatic alone would've looked like thin content. Dictionary alone wouldn't scale. Editorial alone wouldn't compound fast enough. The three together is what made it a moat.

The hard constraint was SEBI. Every public word on a bond platform has to clear compliance. At 4 blogs a day, manual review was going to be the bottleneck that killed the whole plan.

## Execution

- Published 350+ blogs at a 4-a-day cadence from January 2026, enabled by an AI compliance agent I built on Claude that automated the SEBI review before publish.
- Shipped 200+ Bond Dictionary entries, each anchored to a high-intent search term and cross-linked back to related bonds and categories.
- Launched 26,000+ programmatic bond detail pages, one per ISIN, templated so each page carried genuinely unique data: issuer, coupon, maturity, rating, secondary market activity.
- Technical SEO work: schema.org markup across all page types, JavaScript rendering audits for crawlability, sitemap architecture, GSC setup from scratch.
- Set up a daily performance feedback loop specifically for deal detail pages, so we could see which bonds were pulling traffic and reinforce them.

## Outcome

Daily organic clicks roughly doubled in 3 months. The site started ranking for high-intent non-brand queries that typically go to legacy platforms, including "hedge funds in india" at #3. ISIN-level queries, which usually only show up in institutional research, started pulling retail traffic.

Qualitatively, the engine shifted internal belief. Marketing stopped treating SEO as a side project and started treating it as a channel with its own roadmap. The founder now talks about SEO as part of BondScanner's defensibility, not just a traffic source.

## Reflection

I'd set up a page-level freshness re-fetch policy in v1. Right now some programmatic pages carry rating data that's months old. It isn't a live SEO problem yet, but it will be once Google's quality signals catch up. Also, I'd instrument crawl budget and per-page click yield from day one. We were flying on headline metrics for the first two months when per-page economics would have told us earlier which pillars to double down on.

## What I owned vs. team owned

- **I owned:** The three-pillar strategy, compliance AI agent spec and build, programmatic page templating logic, technical SEO scope, measurement setup, founder narrative.
- **Team owned:** Content writing (blogs and dictionary), engineering build for programmatic pages, performance marketing coordination.

# Mobile teardown: /preview/v3 and /preview/v3.1

Date: 7 Nov 2026. Mobile only (375x667, 375x812, spot-check 360x740, touch emulation on).

## How this was tested (read first)

- **The live host was not reachable from the test machine.** The network proxy refused meghgupta.in, so I built the same code locally (`main` at `50a3913`, `pnpm build && pnpm start`). I went through the real `/unlock` gate with the real password and evaluated `/preview/v3` and `/preview/v3.1` from that build. Layout, copy, animation and gating are identical to production. **Not** identical: Vercel CDN latency, and the Cal.com booker, which could not load here, so it shows as an empty white card. What a slow phone sees while Cal loads is close to that.
- "Slow 3G" means Chrome DevTools-style throttling (400 kbps, 400 ms RTT) plus 4x CPU slowdown. Headless Chromium does not model a phone GPU, so smoothness numbers are a floor. Section 2 says what to test on a real device.
- **The attached variants table is incomplete.** v3 renders `SelectedWorkShelf mark="text"`, v3.1 renders `mark="row"` (`app/preview/v3/page.tsx`, `app/preview/v3.1/page.tsx`). On the homepage the shelf looks the same. The flag changes the header treatment on the case-study pages each card opens. So the variants are not identical from Selected Work down, only on the homepage surface.

## The hardest thing to hear first

On a phone, the first screen of both variants tells a recruiter **nothing they can pitch**. No company, no number, no resume. Your role only shows in a 13px pill in the nav that keeps switching between "Product Manager" and "Building in fintech". Then 35% of the screen goes to a six-line tagline. The Venn diagram, the one element that differs between the variants, sits half below the fold with chip labels rendered at **3.95px**. The proof ("~2× organic clicks", "~₹50Cr AUM") exists, but it starts at y≈1110px, a full screen and a half down. And the footer has two links that go nowhere (`X` and `GitHub` are `href="#"`).

### Synthesis

**Paragraph 1.** Before anyone reads a word, the mobile surface says "designer-PM with taste, warm, unhurried". Oatmeal and pine, a real photo, a handwritten hello, a lilac highlighter sweeping across "features" / "products" / "systems". That signal is **correct on "slightly playful" and over-stated on "designer"**. It is **under-stated on the two things that make you hireable: specific and numbers-forward.** A fintech + AI PM with ~2× organic clicks and ₹50Cr AUM should show a number in the first screen. Today the first number appears after a full scroll. The surface sells craft. Your record sells outcomes. The page leads with the weaker of the two.

**Paragraph 2.** **v3 matches reality better** because it is quieter. Its Venn draws below the CTA and nothing competes with the headline. **v3.1 mis-signals more on phones.** Its one extra idea, a dot that travels from you to "I'm here", depends on both ends being on screen. That holds on desktop but not on a 667px phone, so the idea collapses into a glitch. To close the gap in either variant, put a single proof line under the H1 ("Founding PM at BondScanner · ~2× organic clicks in 3 months · ₹50Cr AUM at Ultra"), add a Resume button next to "Let's connect", and cut the hero's 80px top padding on mobile. Then the first screen carries craft *and* evidence.

### 1. Per-audience score (mobile)

**Recruiter: 6/10.** The 60-second skim fails in the first 10 seconds and recovers in the next 20. The first screen (375x667) shows name, photo, tagline and CTA. It does not show role (except the rotating nav pill), company, metric or resume. Experience starts one swipe down and is the strongest fold for this audience: logo, title, dates and a lilac metric chip per job. A recruiter who swipes once gets "BondScanner · Founding Product Manager · ~2× organic clicks". A recruiter who doesn't, leaves with "nice site". Resume is only reachable through the hamburger.
- **Biggest fix:** a proof line under the H1 plus a Resume button beside "Let's connect →" (spec in §2 Hero). **S.**

**Hiring PM / founder: 7/10.** The deep read pays off. What costs points: the Selected Work carousel auto-advances every 4.5s while you read. ### 5. Three things to NOT change in v3

1. **The handwritten "Hi, I am Megh." beside a real portrait.** It's the single element that makes the site feel like a person on first glance. Swapping it for a bigger name in Geist would lose the warmth the warm-intro audience scores on.

### Engineering-ready notes for the top 3

**1. Fix the dead footer links**
- *Why this exists:* two of the four contact icons do nothing. On a portfolio, that's the cheapest possible way to look careless.
- *What it touches:* a component (`components/site-footer.tsx`), no layout change.
- *What a developer builds:* swap `href="#"` for real profile URLs with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`, or delete the two `<a>` elements and recentre the row.
- *Dependency or risk:* you need to decide whether X and GitHub are worth showing. An empty GitHub is worse than no icon.

**2. Put proof and resume in the first screen**
- *Why this exists:* a recruiter skimming on a phone must be able to name role, company and one number without scrolling. Today they can't.
- *What it touches:* a component (`components/hero.tsx`) and layout/CSS (section padding).
- *What a developer builds:* a `<p>` in Geist Mono 13px under the H1, fed from the same data as the Experience chips (`lib/experience.ts`) so the numbers can't drift. An outline pill linking `/resume.pdf` beside the existing CTA, in the same flex row with `flex-wrap`. Change `py-20` to `pt-8 pb-16` under `md`.
- *Dependency or risk:* confirm the exact numbers and titles first (issue 6). A proof line that contradicts LinkedIn is worse than none. Keep the CTA row within the first 548px at 375x667.

**3. Kill the font-swap layout shift**
- *Why this exists:* on mobile data, the headline paints in a serif, then re-flows from four to six lines. The CTA jumps 77px, and a thumb aimed at it can hit empty space. CLS 0.16 also fails Core Web Vitals.
- *What it touches:* font loading and CSS (`app/layout.tsx`, `app/globals.css`).
- *What a developer builds:* make sure the `font-serif` utility resolves to the `next/font` stack that includes the auto-generated `Geist Fallback` (size-adjusted Arial), not Tailwind's default serif. The likely cause is that `--font-serif: var(--font-geist-sans)` is read where `--font-geist-sans` (set on `<body>`) isn't defined. Retest with throttled Slow 3G and a PerformanceObserver on `layout-shift`.
- *Dependency or risk:* low risk. Fallback metrics are approximate, so the target is CLS < 0.05, not 0.
